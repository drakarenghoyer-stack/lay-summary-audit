# Annex V checklist

Verbatim from Annex V of Regulation (EU) No 536/2014, "Content of the summary
of the results of the clinical trial for laypersons". Use in order; tick or
flag each line.

| # | Element | Sub-items stated in the text |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Clinical trial identification | title of the trial, protocol number, EU trial number, other identifiers |
| 2 | Name and contact details of the sponsor | — |
| 3 | General information about the trial | where and when it was conducted; main objectives; explanation of the reasons for conducting it |
| 4 | Population of subjects | number of subjects in the Member State concerned, in the Union, in third countries; age group breakdown; gender breakdown; inclusion and exclusion criteria |
| 5 | Investigational medicinal products used | — |
| 6 | Description of adverse reactions and their frequency | — |
| 7 | Overall results of the clinical trial | — |
| 8 | Comments on the outcome of the clinical trial | — |
| 9 | Indication if follow-up clinical trials are foreseen | — |
| 10 | Indication where additional information could be found | — |

## How to use

An element is **absent** when nothing in the summary addresses it. It is
**materially incomplete** when a sub-item stated in the text above is missing.
Both are scored; the codes below distinguish them only by which element.

An element the evidence table does not carry cannot be scored against the
generator. Check the table first: if the information is not there, the gap is
in extraction, not in generation, and is recorded as such.

## Not in Annex V

DOI, registry URL and citation are good traceability practice and are not
required elements. Their absence is not an omission under this checklist.
